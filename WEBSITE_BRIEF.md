# VidSage — Million Dollar Landing Page Brief

## What This Document Is

A complete, build-ready brief for the VidSage landing page. Every section, every headline, every
objection, every CTA is specified. Hand this to a designer or developer and they should be able to
build the page without a single follow-up question.

**The one job of this page**: Convert a skeptical visitor — who has already dismissed five other
"YouTube AI tools" — into a $29 buyer in under 3 minutes.

---

## The Core Conversion Problem

The visitor arriving at this page is not naive. They have tried Eightify. They have tried Glasp.
They have tried the free ChatGPT summarizer extension. They are thinking: "This is just another
one of those." Our entire page strategy is built around dismantling that assumption, fast.

The page must answer, in order:

1. **Is this different?** (Answer in the hero — first 5 seconds)
2. **How is it different?** (Answer by Section 3 — first 60 seconds)
3. **Why should I trust this?** (Answer by Section 5 — first 2 minutes)
4. **Is $29 worth it?** (Answer by Section 6 — first 3 minutes)
5. **What's the catch?** (Answer in FAQ — for the skeptics)

---

## Pricing Model

**$29 one-time. No subscription. No recurring charge. Ever.**

- Bring your own AI key (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or Ollama)
- Pay once, own the tool permanently
- All future updates included
- No cloud backend — your data stays on your machine

This pricing model is a differentiator and must be stated clearly and repeatedly. It removes the
#1 objection ("not another monthly fee") before it's voiced.

## Part 9: Objection Handling (FAQ)

**Goal**: Preemptively kill every objection before it reaches the checkout page. This section
is critical for the skeptical, high-intent buyer who is 80% ready but has one blocking question.

### Section header
```
[H2] Everything you're about to ask.
```

### FAQs — listed in order of frequency, most common first

**Q: I need to bring my own API key? That sounds complicated.**
A: It takes 3 minutes. You create a free account with any of: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google AI,
or Ollama (for local/free models). Paste the key into VidSage settings. Done. The setup guide
is built into the app. If you already have a ChatGPT Plus account, you likely already have
access to an OpenAI API key.

**Q: Why should I pay $29 when there are free YouTube summary tools?**
A: Free tools summarize the video you're watching right now. You close the tab, it's gone.
VidSage is a library. Every video you save stays permanently searchable. You can run cross-video
analysis across 50 videos. You can build a knowledge graph across 2 years of watching.
Free tools solve "what did I just watch." VidSage solves "what have I ever learned."

**Q: Where does my data go?**
A: Nowhere. VidSage stores everything in your browser's local database (IndexedDB). Your
transcripts, annotations, notes, and knowledge graph never touch our servers. We don't have
a backend that stores user data. Your data is physically on your machine, controlled entirely
by you.

**Q: What if I lose my data when I get a new computer?**
A: VidSage has a full export function. Export your entire library as JSON or Markdown and
import it on your new machine. Planned: one-click backup to your own Google Drive or Dropbox
(coming in a future update, at no extra charge).

**Q: What if you shut down?**
A: VidSage is a Chrome extension that runs entirely locally. If we shut down tomorrow, the
extension keeps working. You don't need our servers for anything. The only thing that would
break is future updates — and you'd still have everything you'd ever saved.

**Q: I already use Glasp / Eightify / NoteGPT. Why would I switch?**
A: You don't have to. Those tools are great for instant, one-shot summaries. Keep using them
for that. VidSage is for systematic researchers who watch YouTube over weeks or months on a
topic and need those insights to compound. If you only want a quick summary of the video
you're watching right now, you don't need VidSage.

**Q: Does it work with all YouTube videos?**
A: VidSage works on any YouTube video that has a transcript available — which is the vast
majority of English-language videos (either manual or auto-generated captions). Videos with no
captions at all (rare) will show a "transcript unavailable" status.

**Q: Do I need a Chrome account or Google account?**
A: No. VidSage works with no account at all. You buy it once, install it, and use it.

**Q: What if I want a refund?**
A: 30-day no-questions-asked refund. Email [support email]. We'll process it within 24 hours.

**Q: Will the price go up?**
A: Current buyers lock in the $29 price forever. We may raise the price for new buyers as we
add features. Early adopters always pay less.

## Part 12: Copy Rules

### The headline formula
Lead with the user's pain or transformation, not the product's name or category.

Good: "Stop forgetting what you learn."
Bad: "The AI-powered YouTube transcript tool."

### The feature description formula
Feature name → What it does → Why it matters.

Good: "Segment-level search — find any sentence in any video you've saved — instantly."
Bad: "Powerful search functionality."

### Words to never use on this page
- Powerful
- Seamless
- Robust
- Cutting-edge
- Game-changer
- Intuitive
- Revolutionary
- Best-in-class
- Next-level
- Unlock (overused in SaaS)

### Words to use freely
- Research
- Library
- Permanent
- Connected
- Yours
- Local
- Once
- Find
- Save
- Remember

### Tone
Direct. Confident. Slightly challenger-brand. We know the market is saturated — acknowledge it
and dismiss it in the same breath. Never defensive. Never apologetic.

## Part 13: Visual Design System

### Colour

| Role | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | `#4F46E5` | Buttons, links, active states |
| Primary hover | `#3730A3` | Button hover |
| Primary light | `#EEF2FF` | Section backgrounds, card tints |
| Accent / graph | `#F59E0B` | Knowledge graph nodes, highlights |
| Dark | `#111827` | Hero sections, final CTA |
| Body text | `#374151` | All body copy |
| Heading | `#111827` | All headings |
| Border | `#E5E7EB` | Cards, dividers |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Backgrounds |

### Typography

- **Headings**: Inter 700. H1: 56px desktop / 36px mobile. H2: 40px / 28px. H3: 28px / 20px.
- **Body**: Inter 400/500. 17px desktop / 16px mobile. Line height 1.75.
- **Labels / badges**: Inter 600, 12px, uppercase, 0.08em letter spacing.
- **Code / transcript samples**: JetBrains Mono, 14px.

### Imagery rules

1. **No stock photos of people looking at laptops.** Ever.
2. **All screenshots must be real product screenshots.** No mockups of features that don't exist.
3. **The Knowledge Graph screenshot is the #1 most important asset.** Invest in making it look
   stunning — dark background, glowing edges, crisp node labels.
4. **Annotate screenshots with callout labels** — don't let the visitor guess what they're
   looking at.
5. **GIFs over static images for dynamic features** — the cross-video analysis and knowledge
   graph building are motion features. Show them in motion.

### Spacing and layout

- Max content width: 1200px
- Section padding: 96px top/bottom desktop, 64px mobile
- Card gap: 24px
- No more than 65 characters per line for body copy
- Every section has clear visual separation — never let two sections bleed together

## Part 14: Technical Requirements

### Stack
- **Framework**: Next.js 15 (App Router) or Astro (if no dynamic content needed)
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS
- **Hosting**: Vercel
- **Analytics**: Plausible (no cookie banner required — GDPR safe out of the box)
- **Payments**: Lemon Squeezy or Gumroad (simpler than Stripe for a one-time extension sale)

### Performance targets
- Lighthouse performance: 95+
- LCP < 2.0s on mobile
- CLS < 0.1
- No layout shift from font loading (use `font-display: swap`)

### Conversion tracking
Every CTA click fires an analytics event:

```javascript
plausible('CTA Click', {
  props: {
    section: 'hero' | 'how_it_works' | 'differentiator' | 'comparison' | 'final_cta' | 'nav',
    device: 'mobile' | 'desktop'
  }
})
```

Track separately:
- `Buy Button Click` (any click on the buy CTA)
- `Checkout Complete` (post-payment redirect)
- `FAQ Expand` (which questions get opened most)
